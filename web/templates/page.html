How to make a meme

  1. Pick a meme template from the gallery above, search for one by name, or upload your own image.
  2. Add your captions in the text boxes. Drag the text anywhere on the image and change its size.
  3. Style it with the classic Impact font or another typeface, and pick text and outline colours.
  4. Download or share: save the meme as a PNG, copy it to your clipboard, or sign in to get a short share link and post it to the meme feed.

A free meme maker that works in your browser

Meme Forge is a free online meme generator. There is nothing to install and no watermark: your meme is drawn right in your browser, so it is ready to download instantly. Sign in with Google to save memes, collect likes, favourite templates and add new templates for everyone to use.
